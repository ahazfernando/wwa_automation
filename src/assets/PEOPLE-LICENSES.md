# Staffing models

The staffing scene uses the light GLB versions of **Businessman** and **Casual Woman** by Grab3D, released under CC0 1.0 (public domain, commercial use allowed, no attribution required).

- https://grab3d.com/models/characters/businessman/
- https://grab3d.com/models/characters/woman/
- https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Original materials and embedded textures are retained. These are illustrative service visuals, not portraits of Clyra employees.
